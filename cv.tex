% ========== PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY ==========
\section{Professional Summary}
\small
\setlength{\parskip}{0.5em}
Most of my work is finding bugs in code that holds other people's money, then writing the proof that stops anyone from waving them away.

Across roughly 60 bug bounty programs on HackenProof, Cantina, Bugcrowd, Intigriti and Immunefi --- 20 of them smart contract audits --- the findings I am proudest of are arithmetic ones. An underflow in Frax's sfrxETH withdrawal path, where a cached \texttt{totalAssets} drifts against the live figure and strands LP redemptions. A payout path in tokenize.it where a caller-supplied base price lets a coininvestor walk off with an entire exit. Both ship as runnable Foundry proof-of-concepts, the sfrxETH one against a live mainnet fork. I run the audits with AI agents in parallel lanes and then verify every claim myself.

A large share of that work ends with me not filing. Three of my own reports were closed by triagers, two of them as intended behaviour, so I work to a written gate that stops me filing anything a privileged role could reach, and I built the tooling around it: \texttt{c0bw3b}, a 13,000-line Python pipeline with 31 modules and 107 passing tests, MIT licensed and installed as a CLI.

I build as much as I break. C. Sambu Farm Manager replaced the physical payroll workbook of a tea estate running 20--30 pluckers and 12--39 tonnes of green leaf a month, and now prices piece-rate, day-rate and prorated pay in one engine. Maziwa Coop (\href{https://maziwa-coop.pages.dev}{maziwa-coop.pages.dev}) sends dairy farmers their delivery confirmation by SMS and generates the month-end statements they used to write by hand. The CRUD was never the hard part --- the domain was: a feed-ration solver built on KALRO nutrient tables, tea factory rates that change by effective date, and dates that stay correct in EAT. I also take paid incident response and forensics on WordPress estates, and build lead pipelines on Cloudflare.

I run teams across media, education and tech, so I can sit with a farmer over their deduction statement and with an auditor who thinks their finding will not pay.
